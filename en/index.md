Documentation on a specific situation: the owner of a private strip of land adjoining a residential urbanización in Torrevieja (Alicante, Spain) is using it as a dump for construction and demolition waste (escombros, RCD). Everything neighbours need to understand that this is illegal — and to stop it — is collected here.

> This is not legal advice: it is a summary of the regulations and administrative practice as of September 2026, with links to primary sources.

## The essentials in one minute

- **It is illegal** on three independent grounds: Torrevieja's Ordenanza de Aseo Urbano (municipal cleanliness by-law) expressly bans dumping construction waste on plots, public **or private**; national waste law (Ley 7/2022) requires an authorisation from the Generalitat Valenciana to receive third-party waste; and the Valencian planning law (TRLOTUP) requires a planning licence even for stockpiling debris and levelling the ground.
- **Risks for neighbours:** construction dust every day; **asbestos** (fibre-cement roofing, "uralita" — hazardous waste LER 17 06 05*); **lead** from old paints, pipes and solder, which ends up in the dust when debris is crushed and is especially dangerous for children (waste containing hazardous substances: LER 17 01 06*, 17 09 03*); lorries, noise, rodents; loss of property value.
- **Penalties:** €2,001 to €100,000 (serious infringement); €600,001 to €3,500,000 where hazardous waste (asbestos, lead) is involved. Plus the obligation to remove everything and restore the land at the offender's expense. The landowner is presumed liable.
- **In the Generalitat's registry of waste managers** Torrevieja has about ten authorised facilities, all in industrial estates. No strip next to a residential estate appears in it.
- **Complain to three bodies at once:** the Town Hall (clean-up order + planning enforcement), the Generalitat (the waste fine — the Town Hall has no power to impose it), and SEPRONA (Guardia Civil environmental unit).

## Who to call right now

| Situation | Where |
|---|---|
| A lorry is unloading debris | **Policía Local 092** — ask them to draw up a report and demand the waste-transfer documents |
| Unloading in progress, asbestos, large volume | **Guardia Civil 062** (SEPRONA) |
| Formal complaint to the Town Hall | <https://torrevieja.sedelectronica.es> — "Solicitud de Inspección por Infracción Urbanística" + Instancia General |
| Complaint to the Generalitat | `stmedioambiente_alicante@gva.es` — Servicio Territorial de Medio Ambiente, Alicante |
