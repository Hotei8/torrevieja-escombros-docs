**Date of check:** 2026-09-04 (first check 2026-09-01 — see "Correction" below)
**Source:** public registry of the Generalitat Valenciana — https://residuos.gva.es/res_buscaweb/
**Method:** search by Provincia = Alicante, Municipio = Torrevieja; separately for the categories "Residuos NO peligrosos (RNP)" (non-hazardous waste) and "Residuos peligrosos (RP)" (hazardous waste), and for each type of operation (almacenamiento / tratamiento / eliminación — storage / treatment / disposal). Screenshots are in the `evidence/` folder.

## Key finding

A gestor de residuos (authorised waste manager) authorisation is tied to a **specific facility and address**, not to a person — so you do not need to know the name of the plot's owner in order to run the check. All authorised facilities in Torrevieja are at specific addresses (the Casa Grande industrial estate, Camino del Cementerio, Partida La Marquesa and others). **No strip of land next to a residential estate appears among them.**

⚠️ Limits of this check: this is a look at the public web registry for a single municipality; it does not rule out notification-based regimes, mobile operations, or entries under neighbouring municipalities. The correct wording is: "in the part of the public registry examined, no authorised facility was found at this address." Repeat the check with a fresh date before filing anything officially.

## Correction to the check of 2026-09-01

The first run on 1 September showed "Gestores de almacenamiento (RNP) — no records". Repeat runs on 4 September (twice) consistently show **4 facilities**. The cause was an error in the first check (the form was submitted before the list of operation types had loaded). The "no storage facilities" conclusion was **wrong** and has been removed; it does not affect the overall conclusion (strips of land have no authorisation) — all 4 facilities are in the industrial estate.

## How to repeat the check by hand (2 minutes)

1. Open https://residuos.gva.es/res_buscaweb/
2. Categoría → "Residuos NO peligrosos (RNP)" (the "Tipo de operación" list appears after this)
3. Tipo de operación → "Gestores de almacenamiento" (then repeat with tratamiento and eliminación; then do the same for the category "Residuos peligrosos (RP)")
4. Provincia → Alicante, Municipio → Torrevieja
5. Click "Buscar" → compare the addresses in the list with the address of the strip, and take a screenshot showing the date.
