## 🔴 ACTION PLAN — the checklist (the reason this report was written)

### Step 0. Gather evidence (start today, before contacting anyone)
- Photographs and video **with date and geotag** (switch geotagging on in your phone; better still, use an app that stamps the date onto the frame itself).
- **Lorry number plates**, logos, company names, the time of every run. Record them in a table: date / time / plate / what was unloaded.
- The cadastral reference of the plot — from the map at [sedecatastro.gob.es](https://www.sedecatastro.gob.es), which also gives you the coordinates.
- "Before" and "after" photographs — if you have older images, or Google Earth / the PNOA archive, attach them.
- Record separately any **grey corrugated sheets (fibrocemento/uralita)** — this is potentially asbestos (LER code 17 06 05\*), which turns the case from an "administrative" one into hazardous waste and sharply raises the level of the penalties.
- If the dump has been growing for weeks — consider an **acta notarial** (a notarial record of inspection). It is expensive (~€200–400), but it is the strongest evidence in court.
- 🔑 **A complaint CANNOT be anonymous** if you want the planning-enforcement route to work: AVPT states expressly that denuncias urbanísticas (planning complaints) «no pueden ser anónimas ni hacerse por medios que no den constancia de la identidad del denunciante (no por teléfono, e-mail, etc.)» — only in writing, with a signature ([AVPT FAQ](https://avpt.gva.es/es/preguntes-frequents)).

### Step 3. At the same time → Generalitat Valenciana (this is critical, see section 3.2)

A municipal fine for non-household RCD may be **quashed for lack of competence** — there are precedents ([Rodríguez Cembellín, QDL 65, 2024](https://www.gobiernolocal.org/publicaciones/2024/QDL65/QDL65_06_Rodriguez_Cembellin.pdf)). - Electronically: sede.gva.es → the general registry / Registro Electrónico General (`https://reg.redsara.es/`), addressee — Subdirección General de Residuos y Economía Circular.
- **Check for yourself in 2 minutes**: does the owner of the strip have gestor status at all — the Generalitat's public registry **https://residuos.gva.es/res_buscaweb/** (search by municipality Torrevieja, by LER code 17). If he is not in it, that is a ready-made argument for the complaint.

- **062** (Guardia Civil) or **112**. Online environmental form: https://web.guardiacivil.es/es/colaboracion/form_contacto/medio_ambiente.html — but that is an information channel, not a formal complaint; a formal denuncia is filed in person at a puesto (station).
- Territorially, your case is handled by **SEPRONA de Guardamar**; drone patrols over Torrevieja are flown by **USECIC Torrevieja** — they have already used them to uncover an illegal workshop handling hazardous waste on a rented plot inside a Torrevieja urbanización (December 2025, see section 5).
- Attach the same dossier.

### Step 6. Where there are signs of a criminal offence → Fiscalía
- The signs: **asbestos/fibre-cement**, large volumes, continuing after a warning, disobeying an order from the administration, concealment, risk to residents' health.
- **Fiscalía Provincial de Alicante** (provincial public prosecutor), Plaza del Ayuntamiento s/n, tel. 965 169 897 / 965 169 898 / 965 169 866, `alfi00_ali@gva.es`. There is also the prosecutor's sede electrónica: https://sedeelectronica.fiscal.es.

### Step 7. If the administration stays silent
- **Síndic de Greuges de la Comunitat Valenciana** (the regional ombudsman) and the **Defensor del Pueblo** (the national ombudsman) — attaching the registration numbers and dates of every submission you have made.
- The judicial route: **acción pública urbanística** (the public right of action in planning matters, art. 5.f RDL 7/2015 + TRLOTUP) — anyone can go to court to demand compliance with planning legislation **without having to prove a personal interest and without Spanish nationality**.

### Who files
- **The comunidad de propietarios (owners' association), through its presidente/administrador** — stronger than a single neighbour. Formally, a resolution of the Junta (general meeting) is not required for a complaint (it is not a lawsuit), but **a Junta resolution is worth passing**: it legitimises the representation and unlocks the spending (notary, lawyer). In parallel, 3–5 neighbours should also file in their own names — more incoming registry numbers, harder to "lose".
- 🔑 **Filing a complaint does NOT in itself make you an "interesado"** (interested party) — art. 62.5 Ley 39/2015 says so expressly: «La presentación de una denuncia no confiere, por sí sola, la condición de interesado en el procedimiento». To receive notifications and to appeal you must **ask separately to be recognised as an interesado** under art. 4 Ley 39/2015, showing that you are directly affected (adjoining property, dust, rats, loss of property value).

## The short answer

Almost certainly **illegal**. Three independent prohibitions apply at once: (1) waste legislation — abandonment and uncontrolled tipping (**abandono, vertido y gestión incontrolada**) is prohibited regardless of who owns the plot, and receiving other people's RCD on a regular basis amounts to the activity of a gestor de residuos (authorised waste manager), which requires **an authorisation from the Generalitat**; (2) the municipal **Ordenanza de Aseo Urbano de Torrevieja** (BOP Alicante nº 40 of 27.02.2026), in arts. 21.3 and 42.1, **expressly and by name** prohibits «vertido de residuos, y en particular los de construcción y demolición... en solares y parcelas, públicas o privadas»; (3) planning law — «acumulación de vertidos y el depósito de materiales ajenos a las características propias del paisaje natural» is **expressly made subject to a licencia urbanística (planning licence)** by art. 232.h TRLOTUP. The fact that the strip is private and cannot be built on creates no right whatsoever to use it as a dump — the TSJ of Castilla y León put it literally, in an analogous case: «las fincas privadas no pueden convertirse en vertederos» (private land cannot be turned into a rubbish tip).

The only theoretically lawful scenario on a strip like this is **not a dump but the «utilización de residuos inertes adecuados» under Decreto 200/2004 CV**: inert stone/concrete that has **already been through an authorised processing plant**, with an individual resolution from the Consellería declaring it suitable for a specific site and purpose (GVA procedure 2636, three months). Raw debris straight off a lorry from a demolished house never falls within this.

Penalties: under Ley 7/2022 — grave (serious) €2,001–100,000, muy grave (very serious) €100,001–3,500,000 (for hazardous waste, including asbestos — €20,001–600,000 and €600,001–3,500,000); plus **the obligation to remove everything and restore the land at one's own expense**, multas coercitivas (coercive fines) and ejecución subsidiaria (substitute enforcement at the offender's cost). Criminal liability (arts. 325–327 CP) arises where there is substantial harm, asbestos, or disobedience of an order.

How realistically will the authorities react: **slowly, but irreversibly**. Torrevieja does see such cases through to the end, but over a matter of years: the neighbouring precedent of La Veleta — first demand at the end of 2021, a coercive fine of **€26,813** in August 2025, the actual clean-up in March–April 2026, nearly five years in all. Three things speed a case up: asbestos, active unloading witnessed by the police, and going to the Generalitat + SEPRONA in parallel rather than to the Town Hall alone.

## What we established

### 1. The applicable law: what exactly is prohibited

#### 1.1. National level — Ley 7/2022 (in force, consolidated version)

**Ley 7/2022, de 8 de abril, de residuos y suelos contaminados para una economía circular** (BOE-A-2022-5809). Amended by disposición final 11.3 de la Ley 30/2022 — the latest amendment to the penalty provisions was published on 24.12.2022 and has been in force since 02.01.2023. As at September 2026 it has not been replaced. [Consolidated text at BOE](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2022-5809)

The key articles:

| Article | What it says | How it applies to our case |
|---|---|---|
| **art. 20** | The producer/holder of waste must ensure proper treatment: do it themselves (with an authorisation), hand it to a negociante (waste dealer) or an **authorised gestor**, or deliver it to a public or private collection service | Whoever demolished the house was obliged to hand the waste to an authorised gestor, not to "the neighbour with the strip of land" |
| **art. 21** | Storage **at the place of production**: non-hazardous ≤ 2 years (for recovery) / ≤ 1 year (for disposal); hazardous — 6 months (+6 months in exceptional cases). The period runs from the start of stockpiling, and the date is recorded in the archivo cronológico (chronological register) | This is about *your own* building work. It does not apply at all to receiving *someone else's* waste |
| **art. 30** | Special rules for RCD: selective removal of hazardous substances during demolition, separation of fractions | When houses are demolished, asbestos must be removed BEFORE demolition |
| **art. 33** | Authorisation from the autonomous region for treatment facilities (recovery/disposal) and for storage as part of professional collection; and for persons professionally carrying out collection or treatment | Regularly receiving other people's lorries is exactly this |
| **art. 35** | Comunicación previa (prior notification) for certain categories (producers, carriers, agentes, negociantes) | Does not replace an authorisation for the facility |
| **art. 108.2.c)** | **Muy grave**: abandonment, tipping and uncontrolled management of **hazardous** waste | If there is asbestos in the waste, it is muy grave immediately, with no argument about harm |
| **art. 108.2.d)** | **Muy grave**: the same for **any other** waste where a serious danger to health has been created, serious harm to the environment has been caused, or the events take place in **espacios protegidos** (protected areas) | Proximity to housing + dust/rats is an argument for «peligro grave para la salud»; if the Parque Natural de las Lagunas is nearby, it is automatically muy grave |
| **art. 108.3.a) and c)** | **Grave**: carrying on the activity without the required notification/authorisation (without serious harm); abandonment/tipping/uncontrolled management of non-hazardous waste without serious harm | The baseline classification of our case if there is no asbestos and the harm is not "grave" |
| **art. 108.3.t)** | **Grave**: failure to comply with the duties of a holder of non-hazardous waste under art. 20 | A duplicate ground |
| **arts. 115–117** | Interim measures; repair of the damage and compensation; **coercive fines and substitute enforcement** | Precisely what should be requested in the complaint |
| **art. 111.3** | 🔑 The sanctioning power of **entidades locales** (local authorities) covers only waste whose collection and management falls to them (art. 12.5), i.e. **household waste** | **The Town Hall cannot lawfully fine anyone for someone else's RCD** — that competence lies with the Generalitat |

**More on art. 111.3.** This is not a formality. The legal literature records that municipal fines for waste that does not qualify as domestic have been **quashed by the courts for lack of competence** ([Diego Rodríguez Cembellín, «La potestad sancionadora local en materia de residuos», Cuadernos de Derecho Local 65, June 2024, p. 202](https://www.gobiernolocal.org/publicaciones/2024/QDL65/QDL65_06_Rodriguez_Cembellin.pdf)). The practical conclusion for residents: **do not rely on the Town Hall as your only channel**. The Town Hall is good for an order to clean up the plot and for disciplina urbanística (planning enforcement); the waste fine has to be imposed by the Generalitat.

#### 1.2. Real Decreto 105/2008 (RCD) — in force, not superseded

**Real Decreto 105/2008, de 1 de febrero, por el que se regula la producción y gestión de los residuos de construcción y demolición** (BOE-A-2008-2486). The consolidated text at BOE is marked «Última actualización, publicada el 13/02/2008» — that is, **it has never been amended and remains in force in September 2026**. A new RD to replace it is under discussion — the trade press wrote in April 2026 that «se espera la publicación de un real decreto que actualice y sustituya al vigente 105/2008 que, con 18 años de antigüedad...» ([CIC Construcción, 16.04.2026](https://www.cicconstruccion.com/texto-diario/mostrar/5847398/gestion-residuos-construccion-demolicion-mucho-hacer)) — **but as at the date of this report it has not been published**.

- **art. 4** — the obligations of the **productor** (the person commissioning the works): to include an **estudio de gestión de RCD** (waste management study) in the project; in demolitions/refurbishments, an **inventario de residuos peligrosos** (inventory of hazardous waste) with selective removal and transfer to an authorised hazardous-waste gestor; to keep the documents for 5 years; **for works requiring a licencia urbanística, to lodge a fianza** (bond) in the amount set by the region or the municipality.
- **art. 5** — the obligations of the **poseedor** (the person carrying out the works — contractor, subcontractor, self-employed trader): a management plan, transfer to a gestor, documentary proof, keeping the waste in proper condition, separating fractions above the thresholds (concrete 160 t, brick/ceramics 80 t, metal 4 t, wood 2 t, glass 2 t, plastic 1 t, paper/cardboard 1 t).
- **art. 11.1** — **«Se prohíbe el depósito en vertedero de residuos de construcción y demolición que no hayan sido sometidos a alguna operación de tratamiento previo»** (the exceptions being inert waste whose treatment is technically impossible). In other words, even at a *lawful* landfill raw debris cannot be accepted without prior treatment. Still less on a strip of land beside a fence.
- **art. 12** — collection, transport and storage of RCD; the region may make these activities subject to authorisation.
- **DA 1ª** (first additional provision) — the obligations under arts. 4 and 5 **do not apply** to «obras menores de construcción o reparación domiciliaria» (minor domestic building or repair works), which are governed by municipal ordenanzas. ⚠️ Demolishing a house is not an obra menor.

#### 1.3. The Valencian Community

- **Ley 5/2022, de 29 de noviembre, de la Generalitat, de residuos y suelos contaminados para el fomento de la economía circular en la Comunitat Valenciana** (DOGV 9482, 01.12.2022; in force since 21.12.2022; amended by Ley 7/2023). In force. Its **art. 67** refers directly across: «El régimen sancionador... se rige por lo establecido en el capítulo II del título IX de la Ley 7/2022», and adds a few regional offences (mainly about returnable packaging) ([text of art. 67](https://portalnormativo.es/articolo/ley-5-2022-de-29-de-noviembre-de-residuos-y-suelos-contaminados-para-el-fomento/art-67)).
  🔑 **Who imposes the fine in Valencia** (under the same art. 67): **leve** — the head of the Directorate-General competent for waste; **grave** — the **conseller/a** in person; **muy grave** — **el Consell** (the regional government). Proceedings are opened by the Directorate-General for waste.
- **PIRCV** — Decreto 81/2013 as amended by **Decreto 55/2019** (DOGV 8536, 26.04.2019). This is a sectoral territorial action plan with **normative force** ([mediambient.gva.es](https://mediambient.gva.es/es/web/calidad-ambiental/pircv)).
- **Decreto 200/2004, de 1 de octubre, del Consell** — on the «utilización de **residuos inertes adecuados** en obras de restauración, acondicionamiento y relleno, o con fines de construcción» (the use of suitable inert waste in restoration, improvement and infill works, or for construction purposes) (DOGV 4860, 11.10.2004). **This is the only lawful door** through which inert RCD can end up on a plot without it being a «vertedero» (rubbish tip). The conditions are strict:
  - the material must be **exclusively selected inert stone material** coming from an **authorised mechanical RCD processing plant** (art. 3);
  - the use must have a specific purpose: restauración, acondicionamiento, relleno or construction purposes, supervised by a competent technician (art. 4);
  - for some cases (including volumes over 50,000 m³) a **declaration of suitability by resolution of the Consellería** is required — procedure **GVA 2636**, three months (art. 8);
  - **«La declaración de un residuo inerte adecuado no autoriza a que dicho residuo pueda ser destinado a otros emplazamientos o usos distintos a los establecidos en la resolución»** (art. 8).
  The practical conclusion: **raw debris from a demolished house never falls under Decreto 200/2004**, because it has not been through a plant and has not been selected.
- **Decreto Legislativo 1/2021, de 18 de junio, del Consell — texto refundido de la LOTUP (TRLOTUP)** (the consolidated Valencian planning act; DOGV 9129, 16.07.2021; consolidated text at BOE, last amendment as at the date of reading — 21.07.2026):
  - 🔑 **art. 232.h)** — «**Están sujetos a licencia urbanística**... h) **La acumulación de vertidos y el depósito de materiales ajenos a las características propias del paisaje natural.**» Plus art. 232.a) — «movimientos de tierras, explanaciones... en cualquier clase de suelo». In other words, **levelling the site and stockpiling debris require a licence in themselves**, independently of waste legislation. ([BOE, consolidated TRLOTUP](https://www.boe.es/buscar/pdf/2021/DOGV-r-2021-90283-consolidado.pdf))
  - **art. 234** — the exceptions (when no licence is needed): where the act has already been authorised by an urbanisation or public-works project; where these are ancillary works to an activity holding an AAI/environmental licence; **where this is an acopio (stockpile) of materials for building works covered by a licence**; agricultural earthworks. ⚠️ It is precisely "acopio for building works" that the owner may try to rely on — which is why the complaint must ask whether there is any licencia de obra at all to which this acopio relates.
  - **arts. 264–265** — the definition and classification of planning infringements (muy grave / grave / leve). The category depends on whether zonas verdes (green zones), espacios libres (open spaces), dotaciones públicas (public facilities) or **suelo no urbanizable protegido** (protected non-developable land) are affected (in which case it is muy grave).
  - **art. 269.2** — 🔑 «**Se considerará también como parte promotora a quien ostente la propiedad del suelo en el cual se cometa la infracción, salvo prueba en contrario**». That is, **the owner of the strip is presumed responsible**, even if the tipping was done by "some lorries".
  - **art. 270** — limitation: **4 years** for grave/muy grave, **1 year** for leve.
  - **art. 286.5** — subsidiary recourse to AVPT where the Town Hall fails to act, plus judicial review of that failure.
  - **art. 292 and DA 13ª** — AVPT: enforcement on suelo no urbanizable for grave/muy grave infringements in the municipalities that have joined.
- **Ley 6/2014, de 25 de julio, de Prevención, Calidad y Control Ambiental de Actividades en la Comunitat Valenciana** — in force (consolidated text at BOE, last amendment published 02.07.2026). The scheme: **Anexo I** → autorización ambiental integrada (AAI, integrated environmental permit); **Anexo II** → licencia ambiental (municipal environmental licence); **Anexo III** → declaración responsable ambiental / comunicación de actividades inocuas (responsible declaration / notification of harmless activities). The **Disposición adicional** of the same law establishes an **acción pública**: «Será pública la acción para exigir ante los órganos administrativos y los tribunales la observancia de lo establecido en esta ley». ([BOE-A-2014-9626](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-9626))

#### 1.4. Municipal level — the Ordenanza de Aseo Urbano de Torrevieja (this is the most direct text)

The version in force: **approved by the Plenary on 24.11.2025, published in full in BOP Alicante nº 40 of 27.02.2026**. It replaced the by-law that had entered into force on 14.08.2024. [PDF on the Town Hall website](https://torrevieja.es/sites/default/files/repositorio-archivos/Ordenanza%20Aseo%20Urbano%20publicaci%C3%B3n%20BOP%2027-02-26.pdf) (the primary source being the BOP).

Quotations to be inserted into the complaint word for word:

- **art. 6.h)** — «Queda prohibido: ... **Arrojar poda, escombro, aceite o cualquier otro residuo peligroso en vía pública o en solares, públicos o privados.** En caso de no existir contenedores específicos, debe evacuarse el residuo en el Ecoparque o llamar al servicio domiciliario».
- **art. 21.3** (the RCD section) — «**Se prohíbe expresamente el vertido de residuos, y en particular los de construcción y demolición, en la vía pública o en solares y parcelas, públicas o privadas.**»
- **art. 41.3** — the duty of the owner or of the comunidad de propietarios to keep clean «parcelas edificables, parcelas edificadas, suelo no urbanizable, urbanizaciones consolidadas vinculadas a edificación, solares u otros terrenos cuya titularidad no sea municipal».
- **art. 42.1** — «**Está prohibido arrojar a la vía pública, parcelas particulares o públicas y solares, cualquier tipo de residuo: basuras, escombros, podas, enseres, electrodomésticos, etc.**»
- **art. 51.a)** — **infracción grave**: «El abandono, vertido o eliminación incontrolado de cualquier tipo de residuos **municipales** sin que se haya puesto peligro grave para la salud...» — the penalty table sets **€10,000 (€5,000 for early payment)** for it.
- **art. 52.a)** — **infracción muy grave**: «El abandono o vertido incontrolado de residuos peligrosos señalados en la Ley 7/2022... o residuos no peligrosos siempre que se haya puesto en peligro grave la salud de las personas o se haya producido un daño o deterioro grave para el medio ambiente o cuando se haya producido en espacios protegidos» — **€600,001 (€300,000.5 for early payment)**.
- **art. 41.3 → 50.m)** — «La falta de limpieza, desinfección o desratización de las parcelas... de titularidad privada» — **leve, €500 / €250**. This is a separate button to press: even if the waste case gets bogged down in an argument about competence, the Town Hall **can and must** require the plot to be cleaned up under this article.
- **art. 62** — publication of penalties for grave/muy grave infringements once they are final (a reputational lever).

⚠️ **A subtlety.** Art. 51.a) speaks of «residuos **municipales**». RCD from someone else's demolition is not municipal waste. That is exactly the gap through which a municipal fine can be challenged (see section 1.1 on art. 111.3). Hence: **the municipal track = clean-up order + planning enforcement; the regional track = the waste fine.** Both are needed.

#### 1.5. Does the classification of the plot change anything?

| Classification | What changes | What does NOT change |
|---|---|---|
| Suelo urbano (urban land) | Enforcement is handled by the Town Hall; compatibility with residential use is required | The ban on tipping applies |
| Suelo urbanizable (developable land, not programmed) | The owner cannot "anticipate" the future use | The ban on tipping applies |
| Suelo no urbanizable común (ordinary non-developable land) | Exceptional consents, a municipal licence and sectoral reports are required; **AVPT comes into play** for grave/muy grave infringements; **the time limit for a restoration order never expires** | The ban on tipping applies |
| Suelo no urbanizable **protegido** (protected non-developable land) | The planning infringement is automatically **muy grave** (art. 265.2 TRLOTUP); if it is also an espacio protegido, the waste infringement is **muy grave** as well under art. 108.2.d) Ley 7/2022 | The ban on tipping applies |

**How to find out the classification:** ask the Town Hall for a **cédula de garantía urbanística** (planning certificate, art. 246 TRLOTUP, valid for one year, showing the classification and the zoning) — an official document that cannot afterwards be disputed. Torrevieja's PGOU dates from 1986 as amended in 2000, with 103 individual modifications; what applies is the «texto refundido» (consolidated text) published on torrevieja.es with an interactive map.

### 2. What permits are required, and could this plot ever have been made lawful

#### 2.1. The minimum set for lawfully receiving other people's RCD

1. Title to the plot + **planning compatibility** of the use (Town Hall; can be checked through the **«Solicitud de Compatibilidad Urbanística»** on the sede electrónica).
2. A **licencia urbanística** under art. 232.a) and h) TRLOTUP (earth movement + accumulation of tipped material/materials).
3. An **autorización de gestor de residuos** from the Generalitat for the specific operation (almacenamiento / valorización / eliminación — storage / recovery / disposal) — procedure **GVA 16562 / SIA 211734**, requiring an operating project, a list of LER codes, hazard characteristics, a description of the treatment, methods, monitoring and technical capacity ([sede.gva.es](https://sede.gva.es/es/detall-tramit?id_proc=16562)).
4. **Environmental**: AAI (Anexo I) or licencia ambiental (Anexo II) or declaración responsable (Anexo III) under Ley 6/2014 — the category depends on the operation and its capacity. **A facility taking in lorries cannot be an «actividad inocua» (harmless activity).**
5. If it is declared to be a **vertedero** (landfill), then the whole regime of **RD 646/2020** applies: geological barrier, impermeable lining, leachate control, acceptance control, weighing, archivo cronológico, acceptance criteria (Anexo II), Anexo V documentation. **Anexo I.1** expressly requires that, when a site is selected, account be taken of «**las distancias entre el límite del vertedero y las zonas residenciales y recreativas**».
6. A **NIMA** number, registration in the Registro de producción y gestión de residuos, transfer documents (RD 553/2020), an archivo cronológico, and an annual report.

#### 2.2. Three scenarios that must not be confused

| Scenario | Legal regime | Verdict for our case |
|---|---|---|
| **(a)** Temporary storage of **your own** waste at the place of production (there is a current licencia de obra on that same plot) | art. 21 Ley 7/2022: ≤2 years (recovery) / ≤1 year (disposal); the start date recorded in the archivo cronológico | Not our case: the waste is **brought in**, the house was not demolished here. And there is no building site on the strip |
| **(b)** Receiving RCD from third parties | The full set from section 2.1 | Our case. Without that set — an infringement under art. 108.3.a) at the very least |
| **(c)** Vertedero incontrolado (uncontrolled tip) | Cannot be authorised at all, in principle | This is what it is in fact |
| **(d)** «Residuo inerte adecuado» under Decreto 200/2004 CV | Material from an authorised plant + a resolution of the Consellería for a specific site (GVA 2636) | A theoretical door, but raw debris off a lorry does not fit through it |

#### 2.3. Could a lawful facility have existed on a strip 7–10 m wide? — Practically not

The reasons are technical, not merely legal:
- A strip 7–10 m wide leaves no room for a weighbridge, a reception area, storage areas by fraction, turning space for lorries, or **fire and public-health setbacks**.
- It is impossible to build the impermeable base with a leachate collection system that a landfill requires (RD 646/2020, Anexo I.3), or to control dust right up against residential housing.
- Anexo I.1 of RD 646/2020 requires the distance to residential areas to be taken into account — right against the fence of an urbanización this does not pass.
- Even if the owner submitted a project, he would have to go through environmental impact assessment, an activity licence and planning compatibility — public procedures that the neighbours would have heard about.

⚠️ **Important: we could not confirm any single figure in force, such as "500 m" or "1,000 m" from housing.** The initial analysis could not either: the PIRCV provisions on distances were partly repealed in the consolidated version following Decreto 55/2019 and rulings of the TSJ CV. Do not quote a specific distance — quote **Anexo I.1 RD 646/2020** («las distancias... a las zonas residenciales») and demand that compatibility be checked for this particular plot.

#### 2.4. Asbestos — a separate and the most dangerous branch

- Fibre-cement ("uralita") contained asbestos until 2002; in Spain its manufacture has been banned since **14.06.2002**, and its sale and installation since 14.12.2002.
- LER code **17 06 05\*** — «materiales de construcción que contienen amianto», a **hazardous waste** (hence the asterisk).
- **RD 396/2006, de 31 de marzo** (BOE-A-2006-6474): before any work involving a risk of asbestos exposure, a **plan de trabajo approved by the autoridad laboral** (labour authority) is required (art. 11); the company must be entered in the **RERA** (Registro de Empresas con Riesgo de Amianto, the register of companies working with asbestos risk); in demolitions the asbestos is removed **before** demolition machinery is used; the waste is packed in closed, labelled containers and transferred under the hazardous-waste regime.
- **What this changes for penalties:** any abandonment/tipping of hazardous waste is **automatically muy grave under art. 108.2.c) Ley 7/2022**, with no need to prove harm. The fine is **€600,001–3,500,000**. Plus separate employment-law penalties for breaching RD 396/2006.
- A recent example of how this works: in Las Palmas (January 2026) a man who advertised online that he would "remove any waste, including asbestos" dumped some 3 m³ of fibre-cement sheets on a terrace next to an urbanización; the Policía Local established through the registry of gestores that he was listed neither as a gestor nor as a carrier; the case is proceeding under Ley 7/2022 as a serious infringement ([Xataka, 29.01.2026](https://www.xataka.com/magnet/alguien-vertio-tres-metros-cubicos-amianto-palmas-pillaron-porque-se-anunciaba-internet-como-gestor-licencia)).

#### 2.5. Lead and other hazardous components of old demolition waste

- In houses built before the end of the 1980s, **lead paints** are common (lead compounds in paints were restricted in the EU by Directive 89/677/EEC), as are **lead water pipes and solder** — during demolition these end up in the rubble and the dust.
- Construction waste containing hazardous substances is classified as hazardous: **LER 17 01 06\*** (mixtures of concrete, bricks and tiles containing hazardous substances), **LER 17 09 03\*** (other mixed demolition waste containing hazardous substances), paints/solvents — **LER 08 01 11\***. The same regime applies to them as to asbestos: abandonment/tipping = **muy grave** under art. 108.2.c) Ley 7/2022.
- For the neighbours this means: lead dust from crushed rubble is inhaled and settles on their plots, and is especially dangerous for children. The complaint should ask for **samples of dust and rubble to be taken** and tested for heavy metals, not only for asbestos.

🔑 **In practice:** if residents see grey corrugated sheets — **do not touch them, and do not photograph them at close range in a way that breaks them**; but do photograph them separately and state in the complaint that this is a **«posible presencia de fibrocemento con amianto, LER 17 06 05\*»**, demanding that **samples be taken**. This is the single most powerful accelerator of the case.

### 3. Penalties: what the offender faces

#### 3.1. Administrative — Ley 7/2022 (art. 109)

| Severity | Non-hazardous waste | Hazardous waste / contaminated soil |
|---|---:|---:|
| **Leve** (art. 108.4) | up to **€2,000** | up to **€20,000** |
| **Grave** (art. 108.3) | **€2,001 – 100,000** | **€20,001 – 600,000** |
| **Muy grave** (art. 108.2) | **€100,001 – 3,500,000** | **€600,001 – 3,500,000** |

In addition (art. 109):
- **Inhabilitación** (disqualification) from carrying on the activity: up to 1 year (grave), 1–10 years (muy grave).
- **Clausura** (closure) of the facility: 1–5 years, or permanently (for a number of muy graves).
- **Withdrawal of the authorisation**.
- **art. 109.2** — if the fine is smaller than the benefit obtained from the infringement, it is **increased to twice that benefit**, even above the maximum.
- **art. 109.4** — a bar on receiving subsidies until the penalty and the restoration measures have been complied with.
- **art. 109.5** — where a muy grave penalty is final, a **prohibición de contratar** (ban on contracting) with the public sector (art. 71.1.b LCSP).

**Limitation (art. 113):** leve — 1 year, grave — 3 years, muy grave — 5 years. ⚠️ The case law on dumping waste on one's own land treats it as an **infracción permanente** (continuing infringement): while the waste is lying there and no measures have been taken, time does not start to run (see section 5).

#### 3.2. Obligations over and above the fine (these matter more than the fine)

- **art. 115** — medidas de carácter provisional (interim measures) as soon as proceedings are opened (in urgent cases without a prior hearing, including where there is serious danger to health or the environment and where the activity is being carried on without authorisation).
- **art. 116** — **reparación del daño e indemnización**: the duty to restore does not depend on the fine and is not replaced by it.
- **art. 117** — **multas coercitivas y ejecución subsidiaria**: the administration removes the waste itself and bills the offender.

#### 3.3. Municipal (Ordenanza de Aseo Urbano de Torrevieja)

- **grave, art. 51.a)** — €10,000 (€5,000 for early payment);
- **muy grave, art. 52.a)** — €600,001 (€300,000.5);
- **leve, art. 50.m)** — €500 (€250) for an uncleaned private plot;
- collection is handled by **SUMA Gestión Tributaria** (the Town Hall announced this when the by-law was adopted in 2024).
- By way of comparison, neighbouring Alicante fines «depositar escombros en la vía pública o parcelas, mantener un vertedero clandestino o un solar sin vallar» under its own ordenanza with **up to €3,000**; in the second half of 2023 there were 203 cases there, 40 of them precisely for illegal dumping ([Ayuntamiento de Alicante](https://www.alicante.es/es/noticias/alicante-impone-203-sanciones-seis-meses-verter-escombros-y-ensuciar-publica)).

#### 3.4. Planning (TRLOTUP)

- Two separate sets of proceedings: first **restauración de la legalidad urbanística** (restoration of planning lawfulness — either legalisation or demolition/removal), then the **procedimiento sancionador** (penalty proceedings).
- Fines on the general scales: leve €300–3,000, grave €3,001–30,000, muy grave €30,001–1,500,000.
- **Incentives for voluntary compliance** (AVPT): if the offender has fully restored the position **before** penalty proceedings begin, the fine is **waived**; if within the period given in the restoration order, the fine is **reduced by 95%**; if after the fine has been imposed but before it is enforced, the fine is **reduced by 50%**.
- The time limit for handling a restoration case at AVPT is 6 months (art. 259.2 TRLOTUP); for penalty proceedings, 6 months from notification.
- 🔑 **On suelo no urbanizable there is no limitation period at all for a restoration order** (after 20.08.2014). In other cases a 15-year caducidad (lapse) period applies to completed works (art. 236 LOTUP/TRLOTUP).
- **art. 264.2 TRLOTUP**: where offences under different protective laws overlap, **only the most severe penalty** is applied. So waste fines and planning fines are not automatically cumulative — the heavier one is imposed.

#### 3.5. Criminal escalation — the Código Penal

The version in force following LO 1/2015 (in force since 01.07.2015):

- **art. 325.1** — «contraviniendo las leyes u otras disposiciones de carácter general protectoras del medio ambiente, provoque o realice directa o indirectamente... **depósitos**, en la atmósfera, el suelo, el subsuelo o las aguas... que causen o puedan causar **daños sustanciales** a la calidad del aire, del suelo o de las aguas, o a animales o plantas» → **6 months to 2 years' imprisonment**, a fine of 10–14 months, disqualification 1–2 years.
- **art. 325.2** — if the conduct «pudiera perjudicar gravemente el equilibrio de los sistemas naturales» → **2–5 years' imprisonment**, a fine of 8–24 months, disqualification 1–3 years. If a risk of serious harm to human health has been created, the prison sentence falls in the upper half of the range, and may be raised by one degree.
- **art. 326.1** — the same penalties for those who «recojan, transporten, valoricen, transformen, eliminen o aprovechen residuos, o **no controlen o vigilen adecuadamente tales actividades**, de modo que causen o puedan causar daños sustanciales...». 🔑 **This covers our case directly**: receiving and stockpiling other people's waste without control.
- **art. 326.2** — transporting «una cantidad no desdeñable de residuos» in breach of EU law → 3 months to 1 year in prison or a fine of 6–18 months.
- **art. 326 bis** — operating installations carrying on hazardous activities or storage.
- **art. 327** — the **aggravating circumstances** (raising the penalty by one degree), which now also apply to waste cases: (a) the activity is carried on **clandestinely, without the required administrative authorisation**; (b) **disobedience of express orders of the administration** to stop or put things right; (c) falsifying or concealing environmental information; (d) obstructing inspection; (e) a risk of irreversible or catastrophic damage.
- **art. 328** — criminal liability of **legal persons** for the offences in this chapter. ⚠️ The initial analysis started from the old numbering (before 2015, "deposits and tips of toxic waste" were in art. 328.1) — **that is no longer so**; the offence has moved to art. 326.1.

**When it becomes a criminal matter, in practice:** the combination of (1) the absence of any authorisation + (2) continuing after a written warning from the administration + (3) hazardous materials (asbestos) or a substantial volume. Then art. 326.1 operates together with art. 327.a) and b) — and the penalty is raised by one degree.

### 4. Where and how to complain — in descending order of effectiveness

*(the specifics are in the checklist at the top; the legal fine points are here)*

#### 4.1. Anonymity and the status of the complainant

- **A planning complaint must be signed, in writing, in your own name.** AVPT: «no pueden ser anónimas ni hacerse por medios que no den constancia de la identidad del denunciante (no por teléfono, e-mail, etc.)».
- **art. 62.5 Ley 39/2015**: «**La presentación de una denuncia no confiere, por sí sola, la condición de interesado en el procedimiento**». The Supreme Court does, however, recognise that a complainant has standing to take part and to receive an answer where satisfying his claims could have a positive effect on his legal position (STS 21.07.1995 and later decisions). To become an **interesado** under art. 4 Ley 39/2015 you must **ask expressly** and give reasons: adjoining property, dust, rodents, smell, loss of value.
- 🔑 **Withdrawing a complaint cancels nothing**: proceedings over a planning infringement are opened **de oficio** (of the administration's own motion), and the administration is obliged to pursue them (AVPT FAQ). This protects residents against pressure to "withdraw the complaint".

#### 4.2. Acción pública

- **The planning acción pública** does work: art. 5.f Real Decreto Legislativo 7/2015 (TR Ley de Suelo) + TRLOTUP. AVPT puts it thus: «En urbanismo rige la "acción pública", en virtud de la cual **cualquier persona** puede dirigirse a la administración competente... **sin necesidad de acreditar un interés legítimo**».
- **The acción pública in Ley 6/2014 CV** (environmental permits for activities) — Disposición adicional: «Será pública la acción para exigir ante los órganos administrativos y los tribunales la observancia de lo establecido en esta ley».
- **The environmental acción popular under Ley 27/2006** — ⚠️ **narrower**: it belongs to **non-profit legal persons** whose objects include environmental protection, incorporated **at least 2 years** before filing and active in the area affected (art. 23). An individual cannot use it — but an individual can use the right of **access to environmental information** (arts. 5–13), which is useful in practice: you can ask the Town Hall and the Generalitat whether there is a licence or authorisation for this plot.
- **Nationality is not required.** Foreign residents of Torrevieja have the same rights. The only practical requirement is a digital certificate / Cl@ve for the sede electrónica (or filing a paper instancia at the Town Hall's Registro General).

#### 4.3. The comunidad de propietarios

- Action may be taken by the **presidente** (the legal representative of the comunidad under Ley 49/1960 de Propiedad Horizontal, the commonhold act) or by the **administrador** under a power of attorney.
- A Junta resolution is not formally required for a complaint, but it **is needed** in order to: (a) remove any question about representation, (b) approve the expenditure (notary, lawyer, expert reports), (c) bring possible court proceedings.
- **Tactics:** the comunidad files officially, and 3–5 neighbours file personally as individuals. Different incoming registry numbers at different bodies make the case harder to "lose" and easier to prove inaction later on.

#### 4.4. The civil route (in parallel, not instead)

- **art. 1902 CC** — general non-contractual liability for damage.
- **art. 1908.2 CC** — liability of owners for damage from «humos excesivos, que sean nocivos a las personas o a las propiedades» (excessive fumes harmful to persons or property).
- **Acción negatoria de inmisiones** (an action to stop nuisance emissions) — outside Catalonia it is not expressly codified but is derived by the courts from arts. 1902, 590 and 1908 CC: «la propiedad no puede llegar más allá de lo que el respeto al vecino determina». It applies to dust, smells, noise and rodents.
- What has to be proved: causation, actual harm (medical certificates in cases of asthma or allergy; a valuer's report on the loss of value; photographs of rodents/dust), and that you asked for it to stop.
- Realistically: this is slow and costly, but it is useful as **leverage** — a burofax (a Correos certified letter with proof of content and delivery) from the comunidad's lawyer to the owner of the strip citing arts. 1902/1908 CC often works faster than the administrative machine.

### 5. Precedents — what actually happens in Alicante and Torrevieja

#### 5.1. Torrevieja, La Veleta — the closest precedent in substance (note the timescales!)
A plot of some 9,500 m² of a former sports complex in the prestigious La Veleta urbanización, overgrown, full of waste, unfenced. The chronology:
- end of **2021** — the Town Hall's first demand to clear and fence it;
- complaints from neighbouring comunidades de vecinos, repeated Policía Local reports, objections and appeals from the owner's lawyers, all rejected by the technical services;
- the owner won a case in the Juzgado de lo Contencioso in Elche, which **paralysed enforcement for about two years**;
- **August 2025** — the Town Hall imposed the **first multa coercitiva of €26,813** for non-compliance with the order;
- **March–April 2026** — the owner finally cleared and fenced the site.
**Result: almost 5 years from the first demand to the actual clean-up.** Sources: [Información, 18.08.2025](https://www.informacion.es/vega-baja/2025/08/18/propietario-solar-abandonado-veleta-torrevieja-120732527.html) and [Información, 19.04.2026](https://www.informacion.es/vega-baja/2026/04/19/propietario-parcela-hotelero-veleta-torrevieja-129234858.html).
🔑 **The lesson for residents:** the multa coercitiva is a working tool, and it is a large one. But the pressure has to be continuous and every submission has to be documented.

#### 5.2. Torrevieja, December 2025 — illegal activity with hazardous waste on a rented plot inside an urbanización
USECIC Guardia Civil (using drones among other means) spotted suspicious movements; the investigation was taken over by **SEPRONA de Guardamar**. On a rented plot inside an urbanización, metres from the Parque Natural de las Lagunas de La Mata y Torrevieja, 12 cars and 2 vans were found, along with dismantled parts and waste oils containing heavy metals (lead, cadmium, zinc, chromium). The operator (47 years old) was charged with environmental, industrial-safety and waste-handling offences. **The same plot had already been reported on 05.03.2024** — a repeat offence. Closure and sealing were requested; the material was sent to the municipal and regional bodies competent in environmental matters. ([elperiodic.com, 03.12.2025](https://www.elperiodic.com/torrevieja/descubierto-taller-ilegal-residuos-toxicos-pocos-metros-parque-natural-torrevieja_1048908))
🔑 **The lesson:** SEPRONA de Guardamar really does work in Torrevieja, including on private plots inside urbanizaciones; reports accumulate in the file and repeat offending is taken into account.

#### 5.3. Torrevieja — RCD tipping in the PORN zone of the natural park
SEPRONA investigated the uncontrolled tipping of tonnes of construction waste on land included in the PORN of the Parque Natural de las Lagunas de La Mata y Torrevieja: about 2.4 ha affected, with spoil heaps up to 2 m high. ⚠️ The primary source we found is an old Información article (2007); we could not confirm how recent this is or how the case ended. **Use it as context, not as a precedent.**

#### 5.4. Alicante (the city) — the scale of the problem and the figures
- About **30 "black spots"** — illegal escombreras (rubble dumps) across the city; in 2021 **3,257 t** of illegal RCD were removed, plus 467 t during special clearance of plots. ([Información, 05.02.2022](https://www.informacion.es/la-cronica-barrios-alicante/2022/02/05/alicante-retira-3-700-toneladas-62356844.html))
- In the second half of 2023 — **203 cases** under the Ordenanza de Limpieza (cleanliness by-law), **40** of them illegal dumping of waste on roads and plots; the range of fines was €210 (litter) → €2,100 (soiling during transport) → **€3,000** (tipping on plots, a clandestine tip, an unfenced building plot). ([Ayuntamiento de Alicante](https://www.alicante.es/es/noticias/alicante-impone-203-sanciones-seis-meses-verter-escombros-y-ensuciar-publica))

#### 5.5. Vila-real (Castellón), March 2026 — what a "hard line" municipal policy looks like
In two months — 8 offenders, 6 of them at a macro-dump next to the AP-7 motorway. The Town Hall spent **€71,000** on removal after being notified by SEPRONA. **All cases were opened under art. 108 Ley 7/2022** and classified as **graves — «multas de 2.001 a 100.000 euros, excepto si se trata de residuos peligrosos o suelos contaminados, en cuyo caso la sanción se mueve entre los 20.001 y los 600.000 euros»**. A police action protocol was drawn up. ([El Periódico Mediterráneo, 03.03.2026](https://www.elperiodicomediterraneo.com/vilareal/2026/03/03/vila-real-declara-guerra-vertidos-ilegales-vigilancia-multas-100-000-euros-127459117.html))
🔑 This is **direct confirmation** that Valencian municipalities do apply art. 108 Ley 7/2022 to escombros, and that the grave range is a working one, not a theoretical one.

#### 5.6. A judicial precedent on the very point that "my own plot ≠ anything goes"
**TSJ de Castilla y León, Sala de lo Contencioso-Administrativo** — an appeal by a private individual against judgment 26/2022 of 22.02.2022 was dismissed, upholding a penalty for placing waste from the demolition of a house **on a building plot he himself owned**, without any treatment. The key points of the decision:
- the classification — «El abandono, vertido o eliminación incontrolado de cualquier tipo de residuos no peligrosos sin que se haya puesto en peligro grave la salud...»;
- «no se ha realizado ningún tipo de gestión de los residuos el titular del mismo... sino que se ha limitado a tirar, abandonar, estos residuos en un terreno al parecer de su propiedad»;
- **«Nos encontramos ante una infracción permanente»** — as long as the waste lies there and no measures are taken, the infringement continues;
- the absence of any proven risk to soil or health **does not release** the owner from the duty to take measures;
- the commentator's conclusion: «**Las fincas privadas no pueden convertirse en vertederos.**»
The case was decided under Ley 22/2011 (the predecessor law), but the offence is worded almost identically in Ley 7/2022. ([Residuos Profesional, 28.10.2022, summary of the decision](https://www.residuosprofesional.com/sancion-abandono-escombros-finca-privada))

#### 5.7. Where the waste should lawfully have been taken (useful in the complaint as a counter-argument)
- **Ecoparque de Torrevieja** (the punto limpio, civic amenity site), tel. 966 702 390 — accepts escombros from small domestic works free of charge, subject to a weight limit.
- **The Generalitat's registry of authorised gestores**: https://residuos.gva.es/res_buscaweb/ — filter by the province of Alicante and LER code 17.
- The Vega Baja has no processing plant of its own — in 2025 the Generalitat announced plans to lay the first stone of a planta de tratamiento for the Consorcio de la Vega Baja, since no municipality in the comarca wants one on its territory.

According to the consolidated text at BOE, this is **art. 232.h) — acts that ARE SUBJECT to a licencia urbanística**; art. 234 lists the exceptions to art. 232. We re-checked against the [consolidated BOE PDF](https://www.boe.es/buscar/pdf/2021/DOGV-r-2021-90283-consolidado.pdf) and against [Iberley, art. 232](https://www.iberley.es/legislacion/articulo-232-tr-ley-ordenacion-territorio-urbanismo-paisaje) — both give the same wording. **Confidence: high.** Even so, the current text is worth re-checking before the complaint is filed.
7. **The exact article numbering of the Torrevieja ordenanza.** We read the PDF in fragments; the numbers 6.h), 21.3, 41.3, 42.1, 50.m), 51.a), 52.a), 62 were extracted correctly, but the numbering of the RCD block (we give it as "21.3") **should be checked against the printed BOP nº 40 of 27.02.2026** before it is quoted in an official document. The wording is quoted exactly.
8. **A criminal precedent under art. 326 CP specifically about an RCD dump** — we found no verifiable decision in CENDOJ. The initial analysis did not find one either and said so honestly. **We deliberately do not supply an invented citation.** The administrative precedent (TSJ CyL, section 5.6) has been found and is reliable.
9. **The Ordenanza de Aseo Urbano: two consecutive versions.** One entered into force on 14.08.2024, the other was published on 27.02.2026 (approved by the Plenary on 24.11.2025, and referred to in the text as «Ordenanza de Aseo Urbano de Torrevieja enero 2025»). We have proceeded on the basis that the 2026 version is the one in force. The wording of the prohibitions is the same in substance in both; the fine amounts differ (for the 2024 version the press quoted «leves 150–750 €, graves from 1.500 €, muy graves up to 500.000 €», whereas in the 2026 version the table has been aligned with the Ley 7/2022 scale). **Our confidence that the 2026 version is in force is high, but not absolute.**
10. **Spanish- and Catalan-language sources only.** The Valencian version of the TRLOTUP and of the PIRCV may contain nuances absent from the Castilian one. Not critical, but it is a limit.
11. **Competence over the fine.** Our conclusion that the Town Hall cannot fine for non-household RCD (art. 111.3 Ley 7/2022) rests on the text of the law plus a doctrinal article (Cuadernos de Derecho Local 65, 2024) — **two independent sources**, but not a court decision from Valencia. The Vila-real practice (section 5.5) shows that municipalities fine under art. 108 anyway. **The right strategy is to write to both bodies**, rather than argue about competence on their behalf.

## What to do about it

1. **Today:** start the photographic record set out in Step 0 and open a shared comunidad folder (Google Drive/Dropbox) with a table of lorry runs.
2. **At the next unloading:** call 092, insist on an acta and on the transport documents.
3. **This week:** file, through torrevieja.sedelectronica.es, a «Solicitud de Inspección por Infracción Urbanística» + an instancia general to Aseo Urbano. Keep the **incoming registry numbers**.
4. **The same day:** send a copy to the Servicio Territorial de Medio Ambiente – Alicante (`stmedioambiente_alicante@gva.es` + the electronic registry) asking them to check whether an autorización de gestor exists.
5. **In parallel (2 minutes):** check the owner at https://residuos.gva.es/res_buscaweb/ — and put the absence from the registry straight into the text of the complaint.
6. **If there are grey corrugated sheets:** a separate paragraph — «posible amianto, LER 17 06 05\*», demanding that samples be taken, with a copy to SEPRONA (062) the same day. This is the fastest lever.
7. **A month later with no reply:** ask for a status update; if nothing is happening — AVPT (procedure GVA 19098) and/or the Síndic de Greuges.
8. **A comunidad meeting:** approve the representation by the presidente/administrador and a budget for an acta notarial plus advice from a planning lawyer. Around €500–800 at the outset is a sensible insurance premium against the risk of a process lasting years.
9. **A realistic expectation on timing:** the first inspection — weeks; a cease order — months; the actual clean-up — from a year to several years (the La Veleta precedent: almost 5 years). Accelerators: asbestos, catching the lorries in the act, parallel channels, and keeping the submissions coming.
